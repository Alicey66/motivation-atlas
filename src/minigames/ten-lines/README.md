# Ten Lines: the climb gets steeper

Motivator: Challenge. Author: rohitr4547.

## Mechanic

Clear 10 lines before the stack reaches the top. Move pieces with **←** and **→**, turn them with **↻**, nudge them down with **↓**, and press **Drop** to send one to the bottom. Full rows disappear. Finishing earns up to three stars for speed.

The toggle is **Rising difficulty**. On, pieces fall a fifth faster every 2 lines you clear, from 0.8 to 0.33 seconds a row. Off, they stay at 0.8 all game. Set it, then press **New game**.

## Dynamic

One classmate played a round with Rising difficulty on and topped out before clearing ten lines. Even the starting speed of 0.8 seconds a row was hard for them, so the stack outran them before the speed-up built. That fits the quoted item only partly: difficulty rose with progress, but it began above this new player's ability. They did not play a round with the toggle off; they said off felt like normal Tetris.

## Aesthetic

With the toggle on, panic was the strongest feeling. Losing did not put them off: they preferred the challenge, and said they would keep trying the game again and again until they could beat it.

## Sources

From the course note's Challenge section: "Difficulty that rises as the player's ability grows (Malone 1980, p. 54) or as the player progresses through the game (Przybylski et al. 2010, p. 155)." The toggle switches that mechanic on and off.
