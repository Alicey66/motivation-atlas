# Ten Lines: the climb gets steeper

Motivator: Challenge. Author: rohitr4547.

## Mechanic

Clear 10 lines before the stack reaches the top. Move pieces with **←** and **→**, turn them with **↻**, and lower them with **↓** or **Drop**. Full rows disappear. Finishing earns up to three stars for speed.

The toggle is **Rising difficulty**. On, pieces fall a fifth faster every 2 lines you clear, from 0.8 to 0.33 seconds a row. Off, they stay at 0.8. Set it, then press **New game**.

## Dynamic

A classmate played one round with the toggle on, then one off. On, they topped out before ten lines: even the 0.8-second starting speed was hard for a new player, so the stack outran them before the speed-up built. Off, the speed held steady, yet completing rows with the shapes dealt stayed hard from the start, suggesting progress here comes through replay. The quoted item fits only partly: difficulty rose with progress, but began above this player's ability.

## Aesthetic

On, panic was the strongest feeling. Off, it felt like normal Tetris at an even speed, but still challenging. They preferred the toggle on, and said they would keep trying again and again until they could beat it.

## Sources

From the course note's Challenge section: "Difficulty that rises as the player's ability grows (Malone 1980, p. 54) or as the player progresses through the game (Przybylski et al. 2010, p. 155)." The toggle switches that mechanic on and off.
