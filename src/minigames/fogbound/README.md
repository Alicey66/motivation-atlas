## Mechanic

Click the game to take the torch and compass and start. Move with WASD / arrows, or hold direction buttons using mouse or touch. Space, E, or the interaction button opens nearby chests and pulses outposts. The nearest chest unlocks Boost: hold Shift (or the Boost button) while moving for smooth extra speed. Release Boost to recharge while walking or standing; pausing stops recharge. Ordinary chests supply charges; hidden relics reward exploration. Each outpost consumes one charge on its first pulse, then opens after three pulses. Both outposts expand torch sight. Opening both wins the main objective and unlocks the treasure-map chest near the last outpost. Quests appear in stages: open outposts, claim the map, find its marked stars, then open the marked remaining chests. The map guides all remaining stars together; after the stars are collected, unopened ordinary chests are marked. Early discoveries count automatically. Treasure totals are hidden initially. Visible markers need no edge arrow. Both outposts plus every treasure earn “You got everything”; exploration remains available. First-win statistics remain separate from later exploration.

Fog of war hides unknown terrain. Nearby tiles are clear; explored terrain and discovered objects remain dim. Camp and opened outposts stay lit. The compass points toward the next outpost. With fog off, the camera viewport is fully visible; all other rules, rewards and layouts stay identical. Switching fog resets the run. Losing focus pauses; click the in-game overlay to resume without resetting. Aim for one or two minutes to win; timing needs human calibration.
