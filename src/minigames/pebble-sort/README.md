# Pebble Sort: a counter or a clock

Motivator: Submission. Author: rajvardhan19.

## Mechanic

A basket holds 30 pebbles: round ●, sharp ▲ and flat ■. The big one is in your hand; the small ones come next. Drop it into its bowl by tapping the bowl or pressing **1**, **2** or **3**. The right bowl keeps it. A wrong bowl hands it back, so no pebble is ever lost. The round ends when the basket is empty.

The toggle is **Move counter in place of a clock**. On, the game counts your moves and never times you. Off, a 20-second clock starts on your first move, and the round ends when it runs out, sorted or not. Changing the toggle starts a fresh basket.
