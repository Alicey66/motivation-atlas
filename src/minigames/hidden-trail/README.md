# Hidden Trail

Motivator: discovery. Author: raycarllei. Human playtest pending.

## Mechanic

Find three beacons marked B, then walk to E. Buttons, arrow keys, or WASD move
one tile. Walls block movement. With Hide unexplored tiles on, you see your
surroundings and retain the places you have charted. Turn it off to inspect the
whole map. The switch preserves your position, moves, and beacons. Restart
repeats the same layout. Compare the two modes.
