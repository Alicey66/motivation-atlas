# Snake Pit: Escape the Burrow

Motivator: Challenge and mastery. Author: jianyangg.

## Mechanic

Collect 12 eggs, one at a time, then reach the exit at the top left. Press
**Start escape**. Steer with arrows, WASD, or the direction buttons using a
mouse or touchscreen. You keep moving and cannot reverse. Hitting a rock,
boundary, or your body ends the round. Pause with **Pause** or Space;
**Restart** resets. Leaving the window pauses play.

Set **Growth** before starting. ON adds six segments per egg, one per move,
reaching 75 segments. OFF stays at three. Both modes share the arena, egg
sequence, speed, and exit. Loop around if your tail covers the next egg.
Aim to escape in one to two minutes.
