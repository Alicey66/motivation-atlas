# Snake Pit: Escape the Burrow

Motivator: Challenge and mastery. Author: jianyangg.

## Mechanic

Collect three eggs, then reach the exit at the top left. Press **Start
escape** and steer with arrow keys, WASD, or the direction buttons using a
mouse or touchscreen. Movement is continuous. You cannot reverse; hitting
a wall or your body ends the round. Press **Pause** or Space to pause, and
**Restart** to reset. Leaving the window pauses play too.

Set **Growth** before starting: ON adds one body segment per egg; OFF keeps
the starting length of three. The maze, eggs, exit, and speed stay the same.
Tight turns can catch a longer body. Rounds are designed to take one to two
minutes.
