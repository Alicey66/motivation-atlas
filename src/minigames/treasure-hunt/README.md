# Pixel Cat Picnic

Motivator: Chance. Author: Zixuan Yang. UNI: zy2786.

## Mechanic

Click or tap fish; keyboard controls also work. Reach 500, 800 and 1,200 points across three levels, each with five hearts and 90 seconds. Eat every eight seconds to avoid hunger damage. Randomness ON hides 0–30-point rewards until eating and randomizes positions, pickups and effects. OFF gives 15 points at fixed locations with repeatable events. Both modes average 15 points per fish.

## Dynamic

One player tried ON, then OFF. With ON, they were more willing to open mystery boxes and replay after clearing a level. Bad-item clicks prompted stronger reactions. With OFF, they did not want to click pickups because outcomes were predictable. This suggests uncertainty made optional interactions appealing. Because several events change together, this comparison concerns overall randomness, not reward variance alone. A single ON-then-OFF session cannot establish whether this preference would persist with more players or a reversed order.

## Aesthetic

The player found ON more fun and appeared noticeably more excited. OFF made time feel long; predictable outcomes removed anticipation. They also felt that fifteen points per bite was too little and lacked impact, despite the same expected points per fish. This suggests perceived reward mattered more than its average. Surprises encouraged exploration. Predictability reduced replay interest.

## Sources
