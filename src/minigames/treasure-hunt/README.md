# Treasure Hunt: one more chest

Motivator: Chance. Author: Alicey66.

## Mechanic

Collect 240 coins in 90 seconds. Press Start, then Open chest. Wait three seconds after each opening. Use a mouse, touch, or Tab followed by Enter or Space.

Random rewards is the only toggle. On: each chest independently gives 30 coins with probability one-third, otherwise zero. Off: every chest gives 10 coins. Both average 10 coins per opening; time, goal, and cooldown stay unchanged. Change the toggle between rounds and compare results.

## Dynamic

Another person played with random rewards on, then off. They collected 270 coins from 21 chests with randomness, then 250 coins from 25 chests without it. Both rounds reached the goal. They reported wanting to replay the random version because future scores could differ, but had no desire to replay the fixed version. They opened fewer chests with randomness, so this test shows reported replay interest rather than more frequent opening.

## Aesthetic

The player found fixed rewards boring. Ten-point gains felt slow, like waiting for time to pass. Random rewards felt more fun: repeated zeros created urgency, while reaching the goal brought satisfaction. Even though both rounds succeeded, uncertainty changed how rewarding the process felt to this player.
